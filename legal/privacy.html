Privacy Policy

FindTime · Effective July 23, 2026 · Applies to BiteMatch and other FindTime apps unless an app states otherwise.

The short version: our apps have no accounts, no ads, and no third-party analytics. We don't sell data. Your preferences live on your device. The little data our servers touch (like a shared swipe session) deletes itself within 24 hours.

What we collect — and what we don't

We do not collect names, email addresses, contact lists, photos, or browsing history. Our apps work without an account. We use an anonymous, randomly generated identifier to tell devices apart (for example, so two phones can share a swipe session). This identifier is not connected to your identity.

Location

BiteMatch uses your device's location for one purpose: finding restaurants near you. Your coordinates are sent to our server, which queries the Google Places service and returns nearby restaurants. We do not store your location on our servers or build any history of where you've been. Restaurant results are cached on your device for up to 24 hours. You can decline location access in iOS/Android settings; the app will not work as intended without it.

Shared sessions ("Swipe with a friend")

When you create or join a shared swipe session, our server (hosted on Supabase) temporarily stores the session's restaurant list and each participant's likes and passes so both phones can see matches live. Sessions are visible only to their two participants and are automatically deleted within 24 hours of creation. No chat, contact, or identity information is involved — only the room code you choose to share connects two devices.

Purchases

Payments are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) — we never see your card details. We use RevenueCat, a subscription-management service, to validate purchases and remember whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives purchase receipt data and an anonymous app-generated identifier. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Data stored only on your device

Your taste profile (which cuisines you like), swipe history, liked restaurants, preferences, and trial state are stored locally on your device and never uploaded. Deleting the app deletes them.

Third-party services

Restaurant names, ratings, and photos come from Google Maps/Places. Our backend runs on Supabase. Purchase management uses RevenueCat. Each processes only the data described above, on our behalf.

Retention & deletion

Server-side session data auto-deletes within 24 hours. On-device data is deleted when you delete the app. Purchase records persist with Apple/Google/RevenueCat as required to honor your subscription. To ask about anything else, email us — we'll respond within 30 days.

Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the effective date above. Material changes will be noted in the app.
