Billing & subscriptions

Purchases are billed by Apple or Google. To manage, cancel, or request a refund: on iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions (refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com); on Android, open Play Store → Payments & subscriptions. In BiteMatch you can also use Settings → Manage subscription.
